// Captain Brann - guards the north road. Gives the bandit quest.
// Variables: quest_bandits (0/1/2), killed_bandit

# start
[quest_bandits==1 & killed_bandit>=2] -> bandits_done
guard: Halt. ...Ah, you're the Crownguard fellow. Maren vouched for you.
-> menu

# menu
* What are you guarding? -> pass
* [quest_bandits==0] Need a hand with anything? -> bandits_offer
* [quest_bandits==1] Still working on those bandits. -> bandits_progress
* As you were, Captain. -> end

# pass
guard: The road north to Frostpeak. Something's been coming down from the mountains.
guard: Frost wolves. Bigger than our grey ones, and they don't scare.
guard: Eskil sends word when he can, but it's been a month since his last.
-> menu

# bandits_offer
guard: Since you ask. A band of cutthroats camps south-east of the village, around the old firepit.
guard: I've four men and a wall that needs mending. I can't spare anyone to clear them out.
* Consider it done. -> bandits_accept
* Maybe later. -> menu

# bandits_accept
~ set quest_bandits 1
~ set killed_bandit 0
guard: Good. There are at least two. Bring me peace on the south road and I'll see you paid.
-> end

# bandits_progress
guard: {killed_bandit} down so far. South-east, by the firepit.
-> menu

# bandits_done
guard: The south road's quiet. Tobin's carts are rolling again. That's your doing.
~ set quest_bandits 2
~ gold 60
~ xp 40
guard: Sixty gold, from the village purse. And my thanks, which is worth less but is sincerely meant.
-> menu
