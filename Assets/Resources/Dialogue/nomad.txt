// Zahra - desert nomad at the oasis. Scorpion quest.

# start
[quest_scorpions==1 & killed_scorpion>=4] -> done
[!met_zahra] nomad: You walked here? Through the Sunscar? In steel? Either you are very brave or the sun has cooked you.
[!met_zahra] you: A little of both, I think.
~ set met_zahra
-> menu

# menu
* What is this place? -> oasis
* [quest_scorpions==0] Do you need help? -> offer
* [quest_scorpions==1] The scorpions... -> progress
* Peace be with you. -> end

# oasis
nomad: The last sweet water for three days in any direction. My family has kept it for nine generations.
nomad: Once caravans stopped here every week. Now only the scorpions visit, and they do not trade.
-> menu

# offer
nomad: The scorpions are breeding in the dunes. Every season there are more.
nomad: Kill four of them and the rest will keep their distance. I will give you what I can.
* I will. -> accept
* Not today. -> menu

# accept
~ set quest_scorpions 1
~ set killed_scorpion 0
nomad: Strike fast and do not let the tail touch you. May the wind be at your back.
-> end

# progress
nomad: {killed_scorpion} of four. The dunes to the north and south are thick with them.
-> menu

# done
nomad: The dunes are quieter. You have done my family a great service.
~ set quest_scorpions 2
~ gold 50
~ potion 2
~ xp 45
nomad: Take these, and come back whenever you need water. The oasis is yours too, now.
-> menu
