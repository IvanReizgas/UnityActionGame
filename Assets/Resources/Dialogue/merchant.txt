// Tobin the Trader - sells potions, gossips.

# start
[!met_tobin] merchant: A customer! A real one, with boots and everything! Tobin's the name, trade's the game.
~ set met_tobin
[met_tobin] merchant: Back again! You've got the look of someone who needs a potion.
-> menu

# menu
merchant: You've got {gold} gold and {potions} potions on you, by my eye.
* [gold>=15] Buy a healing potion (15 gold) -> buy
* [gold<15] I can't afford anything. -> poor
* Any news? -> news
* Just looking. -> bye

# buy
[potions>=5] merchant: Your pack's full, friend! Five's all you can carry.
[potions>=5] -> menu
~ gold -15
~ potion 1
merchant: Pleasure doing business. Drink it before you need it, that's my advice.
-> menu

# poor
merchant: Ah. Well, the wolves and bandits around here carry coin, if you're not squeamish.
-> menu

# news
[quest_wolves==0] merchant: The Elder's been fretting about wolves. Might be coin in it for a sword like yours.
[quest_wolves==2] merchant: Word is you sorted out those wolves. Drinks are on... well, not on me, but on someone.
merchant: And the bandits in the south woods have been stopping my carts. Brann won't spare the men.
-> menu

# bye
merchant: Come back when your purse is heavier!
-> end
