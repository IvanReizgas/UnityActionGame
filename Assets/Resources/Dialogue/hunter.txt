// Eskil - hunter at Frostpeak Pass. Frost wolf quest.

# start
[quest_frost==1 & killed_frost_wolf>=3] -> done
[!met_eskil] hunter: Stop right there. ...You're not a wolf. Good. Sit by the fire before your fingers fall off.
[!met_eskil] you: Captain Brann's been waiting on word from you.
[!met_eskil] hunter: Has he. Well, the word is "wolves", and it's not a happy one.
~ set met_eskil
-> menu

# menu
* Tell me about the frost wolves. -> wolves
* [quest_frost==0] I can help with them. -> offer
* [quest_frost==1] Still hunting. -> progress
* I'll be going. -> end

# wolves
hunter: White as the snow, twice the size of a grey. They came over the high pass at first frost.
hunter: Something drove them out of the mountains. I don't want to meet whatever that was.
-> menu

# offer
hunter: You? ...Aye, all right. You've the arms for it. Three of them, and I'll call the pass safe.
* Three it is. -> accept
* Let me warm up first. -> menu

# accept
~ set quest_frost 1
~ set killed_frost_wolf 0
hunter: They prowl the woods south of the lake and up by the ridge. Don't go out on the ice if you can help it.
-> end

# progress
hunter: {killed_frost_wolf} so far. Keep your back to the trees.
-> menu

# done
hunter: Three pelts' worth of frost wolf. You've earned a place at this fire, Crownguard.
~ set quest_frost 2
~ gold 70
~ xp 60
hunter: Tell Brann the pass is open. And tell him I saw tracks up on the ridge that weren't wolves.
-> menu
