// Elder Maren - Greenhollow's village elder. Gives the wolf quest.
// Variables: met_elder, quest_wolves (0 = not offered, 1 = active, 2 = done), killed_wolf

# start
[!met_elder] elder: Well now. A sword-arm and a red cloak. You're no farmer, that much is plain.
[!met_elder] you: Aldric, of the Crownguard. Or what's left of it.
[!met_elder] elder: Then you're welcome in Greenhollow, Aldric. We've little, but we share it.
~ set met_elder
[quest_wolves==1 & killed_wolf>=3] -> wolves_done
[met_elder] elder: What can an old woman do for you?
-> menu

# menu
* What is this place? -> village
* [quest_wolves==0] You look troubled. -> wolves_offer
* [quest_wolves==1] About the wolves... -> wolves_progress
* Where do these roads lead? -> roads
* Farewell, Elder. -> bye

# village
elder: Greenhollow. Forty souls, one well and more trees than sense.
elder: We were a waystation once, when the King's road still ran east to the Sunscar.
elder: Now the merchants come less, and the forest comes closer every year.
-> menu

# roads
elder: North, the path climbs to Frostpeak Pass. Eskil the hunter keeps a camp up there.
elder: East, the trees thin into the Sunscar Wastes. Sand, scorpions and Zahra's oasis, if you can find it.
elder: Mind the south woods. Bandits have made a camp by the old firepit.
-> menu

# wolves_offer
elder: Wolves. A pack has come down from the hills, bolder than any I remember.
elder: They took two of Pell's goats last week, and nearly took Pell.
* I'll deal with them. -> wolves_accept
* What's in it for me? -> wolves_haggle
* Not my concern. -> wolves_refuse

# wolves_haggle
elder: Ha! Honest, at least. The village can spare 40 gold, and my best healing draught.
* Done. Where do I find them? -> wolves_accept
* Not my concern. -> wolves_refuse

# wolves_refuse
elder: Hm. Well, the offer stands, should your heart soften.
-> menu

# wolves_accept
~ set quest_wolves 1
~ set killed_wolf 0
elder: Bless you. They den in the clearings at the forest's edge, north-west and north-east of here.
elder: Three should be enough to scatter the rest. Come back to me when it's done.
-> bye

# wolves_progress
elder: You've put down {killed_wolf} of them so far. Three should do it.
elder: The clearings at the forest's edge, Aldric. Go carefully.
-> menu

# wolves_done
elder: You're back, and in one piece! Pell says the howling has stopped.
you: Three wolves. The rest won't come back soon.
elder: Then Greenhollow owes you. Here, as promised.
~ set quest_wolves 2
~ gold 40
~ potion 1
~ xp 30
elder: And Aldric... if you're heading on, ask Captain Brann about the pass. He's been worried about something.
-> menu

# bye
elder: The Light keep you.
-> end
